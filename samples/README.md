# Sample projects

The sample projects are attached to the [Releases](../../../releases) as zip files.

| Sample | Shows | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Camera emulator | A Linux board seen by a PC as a USB (UVC) or GigE Vision camera | Raspberry Pi 5 or Rock 5B (USB); any Linux board or PC (GigE) |
| SIL / HIL | One MIL-STD-1553 project with a simulated bus, then real hardware by changing the stream only | Nothing for SIL; a 1553 card or a UEI rack for HIL |
| Recording and playback | IMU, INS and camera data recorded together and played back | Nothing to play the included recording |
| Real time on Jetson | A CoreEngine application in real time on NVIDIA Jetson | A Jetson board |

To share your own sample, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
